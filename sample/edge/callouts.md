# Callouts in every form

The five types, alone on the marker line:

> [!NOTE]
> A note.

> [!CAUTION]
> A caution.

A title on the marker line:

> [!NOTE] Before you start
> The title is the callout's label.

Fold markers, drawn open:

> [!TIP]-
> A folded tip.

> [!WARNING]+ Open by default
> A warning with a title and a fold marker.

Other types, in lower case:

> [!question]
> Drawn in the tip style.

> [!example]
> Drawn in the note style.

> [!quote]
> Drawn in the note style.

> [!danger] A title long enough to wrap onto a second line beside the icon when the window is narrow, which is where it has to keep reading as one label
> Drawn in the caution style.

> [!made-up]
> An unknown type is a note labelled with its own name.

Not callouts:

> [draft] notes
> A quote that opens with a bracketed word.

> [ ] x
> A quote that opens with an empty task box.

> [!NOTE]title
> No space before the title, so the marker is text.
